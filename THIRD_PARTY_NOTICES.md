## Historical event excerpts

Automatically selected Chinese historical event excerpts are adapted from the Wikipedia date page linked beside each event. Wikipedia contributors retain their attribution; these excerpts are available under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Changes include removal of reference markers, topic filtering and occasional length truncation. The source page's history lists the contributors. This content is not relicensed under the repository's MIT license. Locally reviewed summaries in `on_this_day_events.json` are separately written with the linked institution's historical account as the factual source.
